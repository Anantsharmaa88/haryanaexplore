# Explore Haryana – Discover Beyond the Ordinary 🌾

> **A Complete, Professional Local Tourism Discovery & Smart Trip Planning Web Platform**  
> Built for tourists, cultural researchers, families, and road travelers to uncover the true heritage, unexplored nature reserves, and hidden gems of Haryana.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview
Tourists traveling through northern India frequently pass through Haryana along national transit corridors like the Grand Trunk Road (NH-44) without realizing the immense wealth of historical, cultural, and ecological treasures nestled within the state.

**Explore Haryana** solves this fundamental tourism challenge by:
1. **Uncovering Hidden Gems**: Spotlighting lesser-known wonders like the **Rakhigarhi Indus Valley archaeological metropolis**, the **secluded pine forests of Morni Hills**, **Rewari's 1893 steam heritage shed** (home of the 1855 *Fairy Queen*), and **ancient Rajput hill fortresses**.
2. **Providing Verified, Trustworthy Data**: 100% verified entry fees, exact timings, facilities (parking, washrooms, guided tours, wheelchair access), connectivity routes, and direct Google Maps navigation links.
3. **Smart AI-Driven Trip Planner**: Generating optimized day-by-day itineraries based on starting cities (Delhi/NCR, Chandigarh, Kurukshetra, Hisar), available duration (1-Day, 2-Day Weekend, 3-Day, 4-Day), and interests—complete with calculated highway driving distances and regional food/dhaba recommendations.
4. **Celebrating Authentic Haryanvi Living Culture**: Showcasing traditional foods (*Bajre ki Khichdi*, *Churma*, *Kachri ki Sabzi*, *Ker-Singri*), world-renowned festivals (*Surajkund Crafts Mela*, *International Gita Mahotsav*), handicrafts (*Phulkari*, Rewari brassware), folk music (*Saang*, *Dhamal*), and interactive Haryanvi voice phrases.
5. **Interactive Geo-Explorer Map**: Interactive Leaflet map featuring category pins, state circuit trails, and direct turn-by-turn navigation links.
6. **Community Feedback & Persistent Reviews**: Persistent review and suggestion engine storing traveler feedback and ratings locally across sessions.

#### Included Real Destinations:
1. **Morni Hills & Tikkar Taal** (Panchkula) – The only hill station in Haryana (1,220m), pine forests, twin sacred lakes, trekking.
2. **Sultanpur National Park** (Gurugram) – Ramsar wetland sanctuary hosting 250+ migratory bird species from Siberia.
3. **Pinjore Gardens (Yadavindra Gardens)** (Panchkula) – 17th-century 7-terrace descending Mughal garden with dancing fountains.
4. **Brahma Sarovar** (Kurukshetra) – Giant ancient sacred water reservoir, cradle of creation, host of International Gita Mahotsav.
5. **Jyotisar** (Kurukshetra) – Holy birthplace of the Bhagavad Gita beneath the sacred 5000-year-old Akshay Vat banyan tree.
6. **Sheikh Chilli's Tomb & Madrasa** (Kurukshetra) – Pearly white marble mausoleum and ASI museum known as the "Taj of Haryana".
7. **Rakhigarhi Indus Valley Site** (Hisar) – Largest excavated city of the Harappan Civilisation (350+ hectares, 5000+ BCE).
8. **Damdama Lake & Adventure Valley** (Gurugram) – 3,000-acre natural lake in the Aravallis with boating, ziplining, and camping.
9. **Kalesar National Park** (Yamunanagar) – 11,570-acre sal forest sanctuary in the Shivalik foothills with leopards and wild elephants.
10. **Surajkund & Anagpur Dam** (Faridabad) – 10th-century stepped sun reservoir and 8th-century gravity dam, crafts mela venue.
11. **Farrukhnagar Fort & Sheesh Mahal** (Gurugram) – 18th-century octagonal fort town, mirror palace, and multi-tier Baoli.
12. **Asigarh Fort (Prithviraj Chauhan Fort)** (Hansi) – 12th-century medieval sword-making hill fortress.
13. **Tilyar Lake & Rohtak Zoo** (Rohtak) – 132-acre landscaped green lake oasis with boating and deer park.
14. **Rewari Steam Locomotive Shed** (Rewari) – India's only steam shed, housing the 1855 *Fairy Queen* (Guinness record).
15. **Bhima Devi Temple Complex** (Pinjore) – "Khajuraho of North India" with 8th-11th century Gurjara-Pratihara sculptures.
16. **Karna Lake & Oasis** (Karnal) – Mythological midway oasis named after Danveer Karna on NH-44.
17. **Loharu Fort & Baoli** (Bhiwani) – 1570 CE fortress and stepwell connected to poet Mirza Ghalib.
18. **Chhilchhila Wildlife Sanctuary** (Kurukshetra) – Protected 29-hectare winter waterfowl wetland.

## 💻 How to Run the Application

### Option 1: Direct Browser Launch (Simplest & Instant)
Double-click `index.html` in Windows Explorer or open it in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**:
```powershell
Start-Process "c:\Users\STUDENT\Desktop\hackathon folder\index.html"
```

### Option 2: Run Local Web Server (Recommended for Live Demo)
Double-click `start-server.bat` or run the PowerShell script:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\start-server.ps1
```
The server will start at `http://localhost:8080/` and automatically launch your default web browser.
